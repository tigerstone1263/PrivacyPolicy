Is it free?

Yes. Start right away without signing in. Chance Notes, used to play steps, refill one every 10 minutes, or you can watch an ad to refill. Subscribe to Premium for unlimited Chance Notes with no ads, plus MIDI keyboard input and custom repeat-listening albums and playlists.

Can I start with no music background?

Yes. Listen and pick your level and goals at the start, and the app builds a training plan for you. Every stage has theory practice, and the Music Theory lessons on the home screen cover the concepts.

What is the difference between absolute and relative pitch training?

Absolute pitch training means naming a note you hear without any reference. Relative pitch training means recognizing notes by their relationship (interval) to a reference note. In the app you solve each step both ways, and the scores are kept separately.

Will I lose my progress if I switch phones?

Sign in with an account and you can pick up where you left off on a new device. If you start without signing in, your progress carries over when you sign in later. Custom albums and playlists you make are saved only on that device.

How do I cancel my subscription?

Cancel any time from subscription management in the App Store or Google Play. You can also get there from the subscription screen in the app.
