This policy explains what information the Absolute Ear app collects, why, who receives it, how long it is kept and how you can delete it. Absolute Ear is provided by TigerStone1263 ("we", "us"). Questions: tigerstone1263@gmail.com.

Delete your account

In the app: My Page > Settings (gear icon) > Account Management > Delete Account. Without the app: email tigerstone1263@gmail.com. Section 8 explains what is deleted and what is kept.

1. Summary

  • You can use the app as a guest or sign in with Google, Apple or an email address. In every case your account, nickname and training progress are stored on our servers (Google Firebase), not only on your device.
  • Your nickname, profile picture, country or region flag, training statistics, ranking entries and the songs you publish are shown to other users.
  • We use service providers for sign-in and storage (Google Firebase), purchases (RevenueCat), app analytics (Google Analytics for Firebase, Amplitude, Microsoft Clarity), advertising (Google AdMob) and advertising measurement (Meta). They are listed in section 5.
  • We do not sell your personal information. The app does not ask for permission to use your location, contacts, photos, camera or microphone.

2. Information we collect

2.1 Account and sign-in

  • Guest: if you start without signing in, Firebase Authentication creates an anonymous account with a random user ID.
  • Google: the email address of your Google account. Firebase Authentication also stores the name and profile photo address of your Google account; the app does not use them.
  • Apple: the email address Apple gives us, which can be a private relay address. The app does not store your name.
  • Email: your email address and password. Firebase Authentication stores the password in a protected (hashed) form that we cannot read. We use your email address to send verification and password-reset emails.
  • User ID: every account has a user ID. It is shown in the app so that friends can find you, and it is your customer ID at RevenueCat.

2.2 Profile and training data

Your account record on our servers contains:

  • your nickname (we check that it is not already taken) and the profile picture you choose;
  • your answers to the first-run questions: training goals and level, whether you read sheet music, music-theory knowledge, daily practice time and how you heard about the app;
  • your training plan and records: stages cleared and scores in each category, experience points by date, streak, trophies, match wins and weekly league tier;
  • the in-app currency, items and profile pictures you own, and messages or rewards we send to your in-app mailbox;
  • the date you started, your last sign-in, whether your device runs Android or iOS, and your device's language and region setting (for example "ko_KR"), which the app uses to show a flag next to your nickname in rankings.

2.3 Friends, rankings and matches

  • Your friends list, the friend requests you send and receive, and the users you block. A friend request contains a copy of the sender's profile and training summary.
  • When you clear training steps, a copy of your profile and training summary (nickname, profile picture, flag, statistics and user ID) is added to your weekly league, and to the hall of fame when your score is high enough.
  • A friendly match stores both players' user IDs, nicknames, profile pictures, trophies, scores and answers for that match.
  • When you search for a friend, the nickname or user ID you type is sent to our database to find the account. We do not keep a record of searches.

2.4 Songs

  • Songs you write in the app are saved on your device.
  • If you upload a song to the song market, we receive the song and its title, composer, genre, price and length, together with your nickname and user ID. Uploaded songs are reviewed before they are published. Published songs can be seen by all users.
  • When you buy a song with in-app currency, your account record lists the song (title, composer and the uploader's nickname and user ID) and the song's sales count goes up. If you then rate the song, its like count goes up.

2.5 Purchases and subscriptions

  • Payments are processed by Apple (App Store) or Google (Google Play). We do not receive your card or billing details.
  • RevenueCat receives your user ID and your purchase and subscription history (products, dates and transaction information from the store) so that the app can unlock what you bought and restore purchases.
  • Google Analytics for Firebase automatically records in-app purchase events (product and price).

2.6 Inquiries and reports

  • When you send an inquiry or feedback from the app, we receive the category, title and text you write, your nickname, user ID, the email address of your account (if any), your sign-in method, the app version and your device's operating system and version.
  • When you report a user or a song, we receive the reason you choose, the reported user's ID and nickname (and the song's ID), and the same information about you.

2.7 Device and usage information

  • Device identifier for sign-in: we store your device's Android ID (Android) or identifier for vendor (iOS) with your account. If the same account signs in on another device, the first device is signed out.
  • Usage events: which screens and features you use, for example starting or finishing a training stage, the sign-in method you chose or closing the subscription page. They are sent to Google Analytics for Firebase and Amplitude.
  • Session replays: Microsoft Clarity records the layout and content of the app's screens, with text masked according to Clarity's masking settings, and your taps, scrolls and screen changes, so that we can find usability problems.
  • Error reports: when the app hits an unexpected error, it sends the type of error and the place in the app's code where it happened to Google Analytics for Firebase. Error reports do not contain your content.
  • Install source (Android): after installation and when you create your account, the app reads the Google Play install referrer to learn which link or campaign led to the install. It is recorded in analytics and as an anonymous count.
  • Technical data sent automatically: our providers receive your IP address (which they may use to estimate your country, region or city), device model, operating system and version, app version, language, time zone and identifiers they create for the app installation. On Android this includes the advertising ID. On iOS the app does not ask for access to the advertising identifier.
  • Notifications: the app uses Firebase Cloud Messaging to deliver notifications, which gives your installation a token. Practice reminders are scheduled on your device.

2.8 Information that stays on your device

Your settings (sound, notifications, language), the songs you write, quizzes and play lists, and whether you agreed to the Terms of Service are stored only on your device. The home-screen widget reads your streak from data saved on the device. MIDI keyboards you connect are used only on your device.

3. How we use information

  • To provide the app: create your account and sign you in, save and restore your progress, run friends, rankings, matches and the song market, deliver purchases and subscriptions, and send the reminders and notifications you allow.
  • To keep the service safe: keep one account from being used on two devices at once, review reports, remove content and restrict accounts that break the Terms of Service, and prevent fraud.
  • To answer you: respond to inquiries and reports.
  • To improve the app: usage statistics, session replays, error reports and counts of install sources.
  • For advertising: show ads to users without a subscription and measure ads (sections 5 and 6).

We do not make automated decisions that have legal or similarly significant effects on you.

4. What other users can see

  • Your nickname, profile picture, flag, training statistics, trophies, streak, ranking entries and the number of songs you have published with their likes and sales.
  • The songs you publish, with your nickname.
  • A user you send a friend request to sees your profile and training summary. In a friendly match, both players see each other's nickname, profile picture, trophies and score.
  • If you block a user, you no longer see their songs or friend requests, their nickname is hidden from you in rankings, and you are removed from each other's friends lists. Blocking does not change what they can see about you.

5. Service providers and other recipients

We share information only as described here. The providers below process data for us; where noted, they also use it for their own purposes under their own privacy policies. Data is sent to them over encrypted connections while you use the app, and they may store and process it on servers in the United States and other countries.

  • Google Firebase (Google LLC): sign-in (Firebase Authentication), our database (Cloud Firestore), notifications (Firebase Cloud Messaging) and app analytics (Google Analytics for Firebase). Receives the account, profile, social, song, inquiry and report data in section 2, usage and error events, installation identifiers, on Android the advertising ID, and your IP address. Firebase privacy · Google Privacy Policy
  • Google AdMob (Google LLC), including Google's consent form: ads for users without a subscription. Receives device identifiers (on Android, the advertising ID and app set ID), your IP address and approximate location, ad interactions and app performance information. Google also uses this information for its own purposes, such as measuring ads and preventing fraud. How Google uses information from apps that use its services
  • RevenueCat (RevenueCat, Inc.): purchase and subscription management. Receives your user ID, purchase and subscription history, store transaction information, app version, language and currency. RevenueCat Privacy Policy
  • Amplitude (Amplitude, Inc.): app analytics. Receives usage events, device model, operating system, language and mobile carrier, device identifiers (on Android, the advertising ID; on iOS, the identifier for vendor) and your IP address, which Amplitude uses to estimate country, region and city. The app does not send your user ID to Amplitude. Amplitude Privacy Notice
  • Microsoft Clarity (Microsoft Corporation): session replays and usability analytics. Receives the session replay data in section 2.7, device information, your IP address (used to estimate your country) and an identifier Clarity creates. The app does not send your user ID to Clarity. Microsoft Privacy Statement · Clarity terms
  • Meta App Events (Meta Platforms, Inc.): measuring advertising on Meta's platforms. The Meta SDK is part of the app and starts when the app opens. With its default settings it reports app installs, app launches and purchases made through the App Store or Google Play, together with device information and, on Android, the advertising ID. Meta also uses this information for its own purposes. Meta Privacy Policy
  • Apple and Google: payments, Sign in with Apple and Google Sign-In, and checking the App Store or Google Play for a newer version of the app. Apple Privacy Policy · Google Privacy Policy
  • Shorebird (Code Town, Inc.): delivering app updates. When the app checks for an update, it sends the app version, platform and an anonymous installation ID. Shorebird Privacy Policy
  • Google's public time server (time.google.com): the app asks it for the current time to calculate when in-app chances refill. The request does not include your account information.

Other users see the information described in section 4. We may also disclose information when the law requires it, or to protect the rights and safety of our users or others.

6. Advertising and analytics choices

  • Users with a subscription do not see banner or full-screen ads. Rewarded ads play only when you choose to watch one.
  • Where the law requires consent for personalized ads (for example in the European Economic Area and the United Kingdom), Google's consent form appears when the app starts. You can change your choice at any time in My Page > Settings > GDPR Settings.
  • On Android, you can reset or delete your advertising ID in your device settings. On iOS, the app does not ask for access to your device's advertising identifier.
  • You can turn off notifications in the app's notification settings or in your device settings.

7. How long we keep information

  • Account record, device registration and sign-in account: until you delete your account. If you were a guest and then sign in to an existing account on that device, the guest account and its record are deleted.
  • Hall of fame: your entry stays until higher scores replace it.
  • Weekly leagues: [OWNER DECISION: how long weekly league entries are kept, for example "deleted 8 weeks after the league week ends"]
  • Friend requests and friends lists in other users' records: until the other user accepts or declines your request, or until their app finds that your account no longer exists.
  • Friendly-match rooms: until the player who opened the room opens a new one or deletes their account.
  • Songs you upload: until you ask us to remove them or we remove them under the Terms of Service. Songs that other users bought stay listed in their accounts.
  • Inquiries and reports: [OWNER DECISION: how long inquiries and reports are kept, for example "3 years after the inquiry or report is closed"]
  • Purchase records at RevenueCat: until you ask us to delete them (section 8). Apple and Google keep their own records of your payments.
  • Analytics, session replay and advertising data: kept by each provider for the retention period set in our account or by its own policy (for example, Microsoft Clarity keeps session recordings for 30 days).
  • Information on your device: until you delete the app or its data.

If a law requires us to keep certain information longer, we keep it only for that purpose and period. When a retention period ends, we delete the information so that it cannot be restored.

8. Deleting your account

In the app: My Page > Settings (gear icon) > Account Management > Delete Account. You may be asked to confirm with Google, Apple or your email and password. If the app asks you to sign in again, sign in and delete your account once more to finish.

Without the app: email tigerstone1263@gmail.com with the subject "Absolute Ear account deletion", your nickname or user ID and your sign-in method. We may ask you to confirm that the account is yours. We act on your request within 10 days.

Deleting your account in the app immediately deletes:

  • your account record (nickname, progress, friends list, friend requests you received, blocked users, song lists and mailbox) and your device registration;
  • the last friendly-match room you opened;
  • your sign-in account. For Sign in with Apple we also revoke the app's access to your Apple ID, and for Google we disconnect the app from your Google account.

The following are not deleted automatically. Email us if you want them deleted as well:

  • your hall-of-fame and weekly league entries;
  • friend requests you sent that have not been answered, and friendly-match rooms that other users opened with you;
  • songs you uploaded (songs that other users bought stay listed in their accounts);
  • inquiries and reports you sent;
  • your purchase records at RevenueCat.

Analytics data is not linked to your user ID and is deleted on each provider's schedule (section 7). Information on your device is deleted when you delete the app. Deleting your account does not cancel a subscription: cancel it in the App Store or Google Play to stop billing.

9. Your rights

You can ask us to give you access to your personal information, correct or delete it, stop processing it, or withdraw your consent, by emailing tigerstone1263@gmail.com. You can see much of your information in the app and delete your account at any time (section 8). We may ask you to confirm your identity, and we act on your request within 10 days. Depending on where you live, local law may give you further rights.

If you are in Korea and are not satisfied with our response, you can contact the Personal Information Infringement Report Center (privacy.kisa.or.kr, call 118) or the Personal Information Dispute Mediation Committee (www.kopico.go.kr, call 1833-6972).

10. Children

Absolute Ear is not directed to children under 13 (under 14 in Korea). We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under these ages. If you believe that a child has given us personal information, contact us and we will delete it.

11. Security

Data is sent over encrypted connections (TLS). Access to our database is limited by security rules; for example, other users cannot change your training records, and inquiries and reports can be read only by us. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security.

12. Changes to this policy

When we change this policy, we post the new version on this page with its effective date and announce the change in the app at least 7 days before it takes effect, or at least 30 days before for changes that significantly affect your rights. Previous versions remain available on this site.
