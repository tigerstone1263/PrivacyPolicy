Is it free?

Yes. Every training mode is free to start. When you run out of hearts, watch an ad or wait a bit for them to refill — or subscribe to Infinite Note for unlimited hearts and no ads.

Can I start with no music background?

Yes. A short placement test sets your starting point, and reading music starts from a basics course.

What is the difference between absolute and relative pitch training?

Absolute pitch training means naming a note you hear without any reference. Relative pitch training means recognizing notes by their relationship (interval) to a reference note. You can work on each separately in the app.

Will I lose my progress if I switch phones?

Link an account in the app and you can sign in on your new device to pick up where you left off.

How do I cancel my subscription?

Cancel any time from subscription management in the App Store or Google Play. You can also get there from the Shop screen in the app.
